# The world: data, maps, the Reach, ways, dungeons, the Post and its roads

Read before touching a map, a data table, travel between maps, a dungeon, the town's growth or the Post.

## Data lives with the game

- Maps (`godot/assets/maps/*.json`, one row of tiles per line) and data tables (`godot/assets/data/*.json`: items, monsters, quests, villagers, the town, economy, audio, the story, the floors, the Post, the plane, the gates, the changelog) are the source: edit them directly, keeping their shape (a region map may be a sketch in `godot/maps-src/` written by `tools/mapgen.py`). Nothing regenerates them, so GUT keeps them whole (PIX-301): `test_map_files` reads **every** file in `assets/maps/` (a rectangle of known `WorldTiles.TILE_INFO` tiles, its id its file's name, its spawn on open ground, its regions the map's size and known to `combat.json`; every portal, the planned floors' too, to a map that exists and onto open ground; every pack on open ground in an encounter region, off the portals), `test_interactables` every chest (its own id, known loot, open ground off any portal, clear of a pack's home, a mimic in a region), and `test_ways`, `test_reach_plane`, `test_waypoints`, `test_depths` and `test_item_icons` the rest. A new rule for the data goes in a test.
- Read data from a static GDScript class with a cached `static var _doc`, parsed with `SaveCodec.parse_json` (it keeps integers integers). Ask the owning class for what you need, by a func named for the question (`Bestiary.boss_pattern(id)`, `Npcs.recruits()`, `Economy.forge_rules("quench")`, `Town.deed_cost()`; PIX-361); reaching into another class's `_data()` spreads a JSON shape over many files. `tools/test_codegraph.py` holds the calls into another script's `_func` (`codegraph.py --private`) to a ceiling that only goes down.
- Rules are **pure static classes** (`MapData`, `Discovery`, `Interactables`, `Npcs`, `Economy`, `SaveCodec`...): no nodes, unit-testable. Scene scripts only draw, move and route input. Tests pinned to the first edition's numbers (`test_npcs.gd`, `test_combat.gd`, `test_economy.gd`, `test_town.gd`, names like `*_match_the_web`) are the record of the rules, not parity checks: change them only when a rule changes.
- The village and the house redraw per tier: load runtime maps through `world.load_map` (→ `MapData.load_tiered`), never `MapData.load_by_id`, or a funded town shows its old self. The town's age is Pixelheim's alone: a villager who names no age waits for the Hamlet only in Pixelheim and its rooms (`Npcs._age_needed`, PIX-328); the other places' people are home in the Ashes too.
- Interiors chart under their town's place name (`Catalog.place_name`).
- A static func named like a GDScript builtin (`floor`, `round`...) is shadowed by the builtin inside its own class: `floor(level)` there calls math `floor`. Name them apart (`floor_def`).

## How the world is drawn and run

- Nodes are built in code; `scenes/main.tscn` is the only scene. `world.gd` orchestrates the play and splits it into pieces (PIX-260): `world_camera.gd` (`CameraRig`), `world_hud.gd` (`Hud`), `world_messages.gd`, `world_fx.gd`, `world_foes.gd`, `world_folk.gd`, `world_interaction.gd` (what E does), `world_stage.gd` (the story over the world), `world_delve.gd` (dungeon floors), `world_bells.gd`, `world_soundscape.gd`, `world_neighbours.gd`. `map_view.gd` (`MapView`) draws one visit to a map and marks what its art covers (`plan`, then `build`): change how a map looks there, not in the world. New map drawing goes in a layer under `scripts/map_layers/` (`MapLayer`'s `plan`, `build` and `set_night`, PIX-359): MapView keeps the order its units run in, the roots, `data.covered` and `took`.
- **The Reach is one plane (PIX-269)**: each outdoor map's origin is in `assets/data/plane.json` (`ReachPlane`); every region's road out meets the Reach's road cell for cell and no two maps overlap, which `test_reach_plane` holds. Moving a road out, or resizing a region, moves it in the plane too, with its arrivals, the packs' homes 8 tiles off them and a waypoint within 30 steps of its way in. The town, the rooms and the caves aren't in it.
- **The Reach is one land (One Reach, PIX-269, steps 5-6)**: `Neighbours` (`world_neighbours.gd`) draws the map past a road out beside the hero's (offset by `plane.json`), a few units a frame within `BUDGET_MS` 3 ms (`Slicer`; `counted`, so many units a frame, in harness runs), stitches the line (`Seam`), and `world.hand_over` makes it the hero's map at the line with no fade, the whole world shifted back to the new origin; there is always one current map (`world.map`/`world.view`). So `MapView.plan`/`build` are lists of units (`planning`/`building`): anything added to a map's drawing goes in as a unit, under the view's own `under`/`stand` roots, never straight onto the world or its actors, and particles it adds are `local_coords` (`MapView._still`). Every tile the open air and its houses draw on tile layers is made with its tileset up front (`PunyTerrain._prepare`, `PunyTown.pieces`): a tile made later redraws every layer on that tileset (~20 ms) in the hero's frame - a new tile id goes in those lists (`test_streaming` checks). The shaders read the plane (`plane_origin` global) for anything that wanders. Past a plane map's edges its kept ground is what the plane holds there (the map beside, or the ridge's rock via `ReachPlane.tile`); a cliff by water, sand or a road stands on `MapView.rim_layer` (Shade's transparent cliff, `PunyTerrain.rimmed`), since his opaque cliffs pair with grass alone; the masks laid round a map hold nothing on the ridge (`Seam.masks_beside`), and `test_seam` checks every edge corner by the ridge. Harness: `seamless` waits for the maps beside, `unstreamed` makes roads doors again, `crossing` times a walk's frames (in a window).
- **A map's ground is worked out once a session (`KeptGround`, PIX-269)**: its corner tiles, crowns, tint and water masks and patch decks, keyed by the drawing (id and variant) and a fingerprint of its grid; `PunySheet.tile_texture` hands out one shared texture per tile, so never change what it returns. `--screenshot reentry` times entering each outdoor map, first and again (`perf.md`).
- **A change of scene goes through `world._through_door(then, down)` (PIX-269)**: a door dissolves (`Dissolve`: the last frame held over the new map, eased out in 0.25 s); going under the ground (`Ways.goes_under`) keeps the dark (`_through_dark`, also the night's sleep); Reduce motion cuts. `enter_map` fades in from the dark only when no dissolve is running; `fades` makes harness runs show them and reports `change=`.
- **A door in a building opens onto a room, never straight onto a cave or a dungeon (PIX-256)**: the cave is down a `stairwell` tile in the room's floor (Liane's room `observatory`, Captain Hale's hall `keep`: sketches in `maps-src/`, rooms in `PunyInterior.ROOMS`, dressing in `interiors.json`). A stairwell portal is a way `down` (`Ways.goes_down`): walking onto it or E facing it asks first (`Interaction.ask_down`), the cave's stairs lead back up into the room, and `test_cellar_stairs` checks every door.

## Gates and dungeons

- **The gates (PIX-254)**: each region's way in from the Reach is shut until the story reaches it, as data in `assets/data/gates.json` (`Gates`: cells, what opens it, its lines; `GateArt` draws it; MapView covers its cells). Leads past a shut gate go through `Gates.detour` (Bearing, the journal's rows), so pass `GameState.world.discovered` to `Bearing.active`/`behind` and `Journal.rows`: a hero is past a gate when the ground past it was seen (`Gates.passed`). A harness run that should walk through one opens it with `--seen maren_tin` or `--hunted <named>` (applied before the first map is drawn).
- **A region's dungeon has floors (PIX-255)**: `assets/data/depths.json` lists each dungeon's floors top to bottom (`Depths`, pure). A floor is a map of its own id (its own fog of war): handcrafted (`assets/maps`, a sketch in `maps-src/`) or planned (`plan`: a seed and a pack per room, laid by `DungeonFloor.lay`, every planned floor's rooms pinned by `test_depths`). `MapData.load_by_id` lays a planned floor and `Depths.dress` wires every floor's stairs to the next (a `stairwell` down that asks, a `cave` stair up), its tint, its own dark (`MapData.light`) and its set piece's words (`MapData.notes`, E reads them; `MapData.pieces` draws dungeon-sheet pieces over cells). A floor's packs stand `lift` levels up. The bottom floor's boss opens the `shortcut` (`Depths.open_shortcut`; `Delve.open_shortcut` when it falls). **A boss's fall opens its way out where it fell (PIX-292, PIX-339)**: `Delve` waits until the body has gone and the spoils over it have risen and faded (at most 4 s), then `StairRise` cracks the floor and raises the stair (`Depths.open_way_out`; Reduce motion fades it); `--then-walk` walks out once it has. Tables keyed by map id (music, the Reach's stages) go by `Depths.root`. No save field: floors are fog, finds are chests, the shortcut is the named hunt.
- The dungeons by chapter: the sea cave (Saltmere), the Blackiron shafts (`shafts`, `shafts_gallery` planned, `shafts_blackseam`), the Greyhold cellars (`cellars`, `cellars_crypt` planned, `cellars_hall`), the ice cave (`icecave`, `icecave_lake`, `icecave_glass`); a shortcut's `look` picks its door (`PunyDungeon.DOORS`: `gate`, `cage`, `stair`, `slide`). A chest may say a line as it opens (`said`). Story details per region are in `story.md`.
- **The Kings' Vault, after the story (PIX-257)**: depths.json `vault`, five floors laid from seeds (`vault_1`..`vault_5`, a vault theme: the `stair_kings`, `strongroom`, `tombs`, `armoury` and `hoard` set pieces, `h` a strongbox), behind a door in the rock on the mountain road (12,2) whose portal says `barred: "vault"` (`Ways.barred`/`barred_line`). It opens on depths.json `opens`, read as a main quest step (`MainQuest.holds`); its first opening plays `vault_door` (`Vault.first_word`). A first floor's stair leads out by the dungeon's `out`. A floor's `forged` tier deep-forges its kills' gear and its guardian's prize (`Depths.forged`, `InventoryState.deepen`); a chest's loot may carry `rarity` and `forged`. The Deep Hunt's four named and the Hollow King keep the floors (`board: false`). Gulp is posted at the Town (`postedTier`).

## The Pixelheim Post and its roads (PIX-320..326)

- **The Post (PIX-320)**: `assets/data/post.json` (correspondents, routes, eighteen threads of pieces) and `Post` (pure): it opens with `opens` (Maren's first letter delivered), its box at `box` (town 44,21; `HamletArt`'s `post_box`/`post_box_full`). `Post.in_box` is worked out from the day (`Gathering.day_of`), `seed` and the threads: each thread offers its next piece once the one before is delivered, only for someone `present` where the hero has `reached`, no gate in the way, its sender at home and its `when` met (MainQuest conditions); a thread may post only every n mornings (`every`) or for one ending (`ending`, `Post.for_hero`); `pick` draws up to `Post.bag_size` (`bag`, five with the post house). `GameState.posting` takes the box (E facing it) and delivers as a word with the addressee ends (`Questing.finish_dialogue`). Saved: `PostState` (the satchel, threads' steps, `delivered`, `taken`, the book's `stamps`/`places`, `routes`, `signs`, `lit`, `hubs`). Nothing expires. Journal's Post and Postmarks tabs (`Postmark` draws each page's stamp), report `post=`, `stamps=`, flows `post-*`.
- **Threads (PIX-321, PIX-326)**: every hub in two at least (`test_post` counts them and checks three pieces a day through chapters 2-5): both ends of a posted piece must be `reachable`, a piece may wait on another thread's piece (`after`), Morvax's post goes where `Homecoming.place` has him. A delivery stamps the courier's book (`Post.place_of`). `gift` (an item) and `recipe`. After the story Maren writes weekly, the Vault's heirlooms go home (`mark`), and the Dragon Post flies the day's furthest piece (post.json `dragon`).
- **Routes (PIX-322, `Routes`)**: every piece handed over counts toward the route from Pixelheim to the place at each of its ends, by who wrote it (`Routes.tally`); a route that has carried a few pieces raises signposts at its forks named after its main writer (`raise_signs`, `RouteArt`), one that has carried enough has its waystation offered on the town's board (town.json's `works`): built, it stands halfway along on the Reach with a hut, a bench that mends you, a post box and an ember lamp, and is a stage to travel to. The map draws the routes in gold. Harness `--routes place:n[:who],...|all`.
- **Lamps (PIX-323, PIX-324, `Lamps`)**: each waystation lamp keeps the day it was lit (`PostState.lit`, the one thing saved) and the rest is worked out from the mornings since: its ring heals a cell further into the ash each morning, up to five (bare earth at the front, then grass, flowers, saplings), only the ash. MapView lays the healed cells over the ground, so `KeptGround` works them into the corner tiles. No night pack rises inside a ring (`Lamps.keeps_off`) and the air there is the open land's (`Weather.air_at`). Each lamp is its region's kind (`Lamps.KINDS`: sea glass reaches further, frost lily heals faster, grave moss thins the Mirefen's fog, `Lamps.clears`); once Aske is home every ring is a third wider. A whole ring draws morning finds and visitors (from the day, nothing saved). As the ash heals the Ash Fields are renamed (`Lamps.region_name`, combat.json's `renamed`). Harness `--lit place:mornings`; report `healed=`, `air=`, `lamps=`, `finds=`, `visitors=`.
- **Lantern Night (PIX-362, `scripts/features/lantern_night/`)**: after the story (`Post.dragon_open`) the last day of every 28-day Reach year (`LanternNight.YEAR`, `NIGHT`) is a festival kept in `SettlementState.festival` with its own `lantern` (the day) and `flown` marks; until Fafnyr's pass the Reach's lamps wait dark after dusk (`LanternLayer.set_night`, which runs after MapView's glows), and on the Reach after dark the world's once-a-second watch (`LanternPass.watch`) plays story.json's `lantern_night` (its cells counted from the hero's), lighting each lamp as he crosses over it; Esc lights them all at once, Reduce motion lights them as it opens. Harness `--lantern wait|fly`; report `lantern=`.
- **Hubs (PIX-324, `Hubs`)**: each place at the end of a post road grows in three steps at 3, 5 and 7 pieces carried (post.json's routes `grows`), drawn by `HubArt` as Hamlet lays a settlement's dressing; the first visit after a step has its own tour (once per spot, `PostState.hubs`; harness `hubtour`). Report `hub=`, `tour=`.
- **The lookout (PIX-363, `scripts/features/lookout/`)**: a spyglass on the mountain road (`Lookout.CELL`, a feature's map layer); the road is off the Reach's plane, so E there after dark opens `LookoutScreen`, the map screen's `Painting` of the Reach's page drawn for the night, every lit lamp (`LookoutScreen.lamps`, none while Lantern Night's wait) a halo in its kind's colour (`RouteArt.light_of`) and its healed ring (`rings_healed`) round it: drawn on the screen's canvas, no light. By day E says to come back after dark. E and its prompt are two direct calls in `world_interaction.gd` (a feature has no hook for them). Report `lookout=`.
- **The post house (PIX-325)**: a work on the town's board offered once a waystation stands; built, it stands behind the box (`Post.house_cells`) and the box holds five pieces a morning. Morvax keeps count of the lamps on his forge wall (`TallyMarks` in map_view.gd; `Homecoming.count_line` after the story). Report `posthouse=`, `tally=`.
