# The story: scenes, letters, the two nights, the ending, the regions' chapters

Read before touching story.json, progression.json, a quest, a letter, a named foe, a chapter or the endings. The world it plays on is in `world.md`; the Post's story threads too.

- **Story moments are data (PIX-31)**: a cutscene is a scene in `godot/assets/data/story.json` (steps of `Cutscene.KINDS`: stage, fade, caption, card, tint, ash, shake, actor, logo, credits, wait, theme) played by `scripts/cutscene.gd`; write or change one there, not in code, and `test_cutscene.gd` checks every step. The scenes played are the `opening` (New Game, from the title), the dreams (`Story.next_dream`), `bells_dawn` and `bells_collar`, `ending_home` and `ending_stay` (story.json's `moments`), and `vault_door`; the old boss intros (`fafnyr`, `morvax`) and the `eyes` step went with PIX-352. Play one from code with `Cutscene.new()`, `scene_id`, `on_done`, then `add_child`, or over the world with `world.stage.play_story(id)` (harness `--story <id>`, `credits`).
- **The Night of Ash (PIX-152, `Prologue`)**: a new hero's first night, played on the town at age 0; harness runs skip it unless `--prologue N`; the dawn after it is `dawn_screen.gd` (`--dawn-beat N`). Sela's cap stays in the pack while the night runs (`Prologue.keeps`, PIX-344: `Upkeep.drop_gear` and `Trade.sell_gear` refuse it), or the night waited for head gear forever.
- **Maren's letters (PIX-253, `Letters`)**: five letters carried to the regions; a letter's answer can wait where it was written (`reading` on the letter's quest, `Letters.reading_at`: Hale's order book on his table in the keep, shut until the letter is delivered, read once into the story ledger for a `seen` main quest step; harness `--delivered <quest>`). An `unbuilt` main quest step waits for a later step to write it while `MainQuest.continued` shows the honest card. Chapter cards are `chapter_screen.gd`.
- **The fifth letter, and the old mountain out of play (PIX-253 step 8, PIX-257)**: the Ashen Mountain's floors, the Undermountain's, its cave and its waypoint left play, and with step 11 their code and data too (`Dungeons`, the floor select, `levels`, the Deep Hunt): nothing in the world, the map, the journal, the hints or Bearing may point at them (`test_fifth_letter`, `test_vault`). The save's `unlockedLevel`, `clearedLevels` and `deepHunt` went with PIX-319, and every guess made from them. The mountain's gate at overworld (48,6) is a map portal `barred` until Maren's promise (`Relics.gate_open`: her confession heard, the fifth letter given) onto `mountain_road`, up to the `morvax_forge` room (the tally marks are a story.json `readings` piece, read with E, drawn by MapView; lamps lit add red strokes, PIX-325).
- **The Night of Bells (PIX-253 step 9, PIX-297)**: progression.json's `bells`, rules in `Bells` (pure), played on the town map by the world's `Night` (`world_bells.gd`, `world.night`). Home with the run home next - through the gate, a save loaded in town, or standing there when the step comes (a once-a-second watch) - the night begins; its beats are chapter 7's `bells` steps (lanterns, embers and roofs, hold), met while `ProgressionState.bells` runs past them or once the collar's scene is in the story ledger (`Bells.over`). The night is saved as it goes (the file's `story.night`, the beat with `bells_lit`, `bells_doused`, `bells_embers`; each beat a moment save): a save made in it resumes at its beat as a fall does, never past the hold (flow `bells-resume`). The townsfolk's jobs are data (`jobs`, each only where its person lives in town); Fafnyr is `Bells.fafnyr()` (a boss with `yieldsAt` and `roars` of his own: `Hunts.yields`, BossBrain), standing down at dawn (`dawnSeconds` of holding the square) or at his share, then `Foes.stood_down` hands him to the Night: the dawn and collar scenes (Cutscene's `world` stage: actors on world cells), the fountain built by his scale (`Holdings.scale_in_fountain`), the day. Every way out of town is barred that night; a fall wakes in the inn's doorway with the beat set out again. `--bells N` (with `light`, `douse`, `daybreak`, `hurt`) drives it; the report adds `bells`, `lit`, `burning`, `jobs`, `scene`. `MapView.burn`, `lamps_out` (their lights freed) and `pitch_tent` set the town; `test_bells` keeps the lights per block within test_night_light's budget. Its fires are `FireArt` (`art.md`).
- **Coming Home (PIX-253 step 10)**: chapter 8 is one `chosen` step, met once an ending is in the story ledger (`Story.ending_of`: `ending_home`/`ending_stay` by story.json's moments `ending:home`/`ending:stay`). Rules in `Homecoming` (pure, progression.json's `home`): after the collar Morvax (`mountain_morvax`, `alsoOn: town`) waits by the fountain (`Homecoming.place`), his gold "!" and the arrow on him; his words (`ask`) closing open the re-aimed `throne_screen.gd` (`Stage.ask_morvax`), whose answer runs `Stage.play_ending`: his reply, the landmarks, the square, Hilda's forge and Maren, then the scene and credits. Afterwards `Folk` places him by `Homecoming.placed` (home: his bench; staying: his forge, his bench on festival days), with the day's `argument` (his side, and Maren's via `maren_word`) and his count of the lamps (`Homecoming.count_line`). The City asks `freed` (`Homecoming.dragon_done`). Villagers are placed before they enter the tree (`Folk.spawn_for`): an `AnimatableBody2D` synced to physics keeps its entry position while the tree is paused, so anything spawned under a held world stood at the map's corner. Harness: `choice`, `ending [stay]`, `--story <id> credits`; the report's `ending`, `morvax`, `credits`.
- **A family the story brings home** is a recruit with `comesHome` (Wenna: her letter delivered, the Tidecaller down): `Holdings.come_home` settles them in the save's settlers (quietly as a save loads), their `home` may be a room (`town_inn`), and the town's tour stops at their door (`settler:` reveals). A settler's `perkValues` add to a trade's share (`Holdings.settler_share`: Pell's `forge`); a timed run's clock stops once its giver is settled. A dream may wait on a settler (`story.json` dreams' `home`). An age's `opens` words play on the tour (`opens:<tier>`, `Holdings.note_age_open`).
- **The regions' chapters (PIX-255)**: chapter 3, the Blackiron shafts (Bram's cheese, Pell's canary in the `canary` set piece, the Seam Warden by a `winch`); chapter 4, the Greyhold cellars (Fenwick's locket, Ana's father's badge in the `crypt` set piece, the Hollow Captain); chapter 5, the ice cave (Gunnar's strongbox, the frozen lake with the boots in its edge, Rimefang on Liane's lantern; Liane's sums are Aske's letter's `reading` on her desk; Aske comes home as the lamplighter, his perk a `lantern` share, `LightRig.lantern_reach`).
- **A named foe can stand down rather than fall (PIX-255)**: combat.json's `yieldsAt` (a share of its health: `Hunts.yields`) and `yields` (`{lines, card, line}`). At its share, even under a killing blow, `enemy.stand_down()` takes it out of the fight (`dying` and `stood_down`: no blow lands, no bite, no trick, the boss bar lets go) and `Foes.stood_down` plays its lines in a conversation after a breath, then pays it out as a fall (keepsake, named hunt, shortcut, settlers home), shows its card and has it `lie_down()`. The Hollow Captain hears Maren's line this way; the Night of Bells' dragon at dawn. The dragon is the only boss with attacks of its own (`BossBrain`: `breath` and `embers`).
- **Getting dressed (PIX-294)**: each class finds a weapon, armour and a trinket it couldn't buy before at each chapter (Hilda's stock by the relics' stage, each region's trader its tier; `test_dressing` measures every class's fights and prints the numbers); levels come slower early (the regions' `wildXp`, the fields' `wildXpMult` 0.25, the early quests' XP), about 4 at Saltmere, 12-13 at the gate, 14 on the Night of Bells and 15-19 down the Vault (`test_pacing`).
- **Forgetting is a person (PIX-331)**: Old Hessa keeps the shrine (`Skills.FORGETTER`): she forgets the hero's skills or training, the first time of each free.
