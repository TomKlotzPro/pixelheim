# UI: screens, the look, the HUD, words on the screen, input

Read before touching a screen, the HUD, anything with text or keys.

## Screens

- **Every menu, page and conversation extends `Screen` (`scripts/screen.gd`, PIX-136)**: build it in `_open()` (not `_ready`), set `closing_actions` for its own key (`[&"inventory"]`), return what a key does from `_command(event)` (an empty `Callable` lets it pass on to closing), and leave with `close()`. Screens *hold* the world rather than pausing it: holds are counted and the world runs again only when the last screen lets go, so never write `get_tree().paused` in a screen (only right before `reload_current_scene`). Open the hero's screens through `world.open_screen(name)`.
- **A screen that dims the world has the whole canvas (PIX-346)**: `dim()` puts the HUD's layer aside (the dock, the clock, the quill, every line over the world) while it's open, counted like the holds and brought back as it was when the last of them goes. The title, the rank-up and every cutscene (PIX-368: a sign's plate stood half under the letterbox) put it aside too; the dawn keeps it (its sky is the HUD's). `Layout.over` finds an open screen's words on the HUD's dock, clock or quill (`Hud.shown`), and the harness's `overflow` counts them, so every `fit-*` flow holds it.
- Keys reach the top screen only (the highest layer, the latest among equals, PIX-199). `Screen` marks the key handled **before** it runs the command `_command` returned: an action that reloads the scene frees the menu (calling it after logs `!is_inside_tree`), and a key must never reach a second screen (PIX-131).
- Screens ease in on their own: the world hooks every `*_screen.gd` CanvasLayer it adds into `UiStyle.enter` (fade + 10 px rise, skipped with reduce motion; the title and rank-up make their own entrances), which also plays `Sound.play_ui("open")` and `"close"`; move keys tick while a screen pauses the tree. Those sounds are synthesized in `sound.gd` (`UI_SOUNDS`).

## The look (`UiStyle`, `scripts/ui_style.gd`, PIX-138)

- Pages of a village ledger. Parchment (`WINDOW`) in a carved wooden frame with brass corner studs, rows and slots (`CARD`) inked on the page with a `RIM` line, wooden planks for buttons (`UiStyle.plank`, `UiStyle.focus(button, on)` for the chosen one). Frames are pixel art generated from the palette at 1x and shown at `UI_SCALE` 2, as 9-slice `StyleBoxTexture`s (the page tiles its grain, never stretches it).
- **GegX's kit dresses the menus when it's installed (PIX-385)**: `UiStyle._kit(piece)` builds a 9-slice from `assets/puny/gegx/` at scale 1 (the kit is drawn for canvas pixels; never through `_texture`, which doubles) with `ui_kit.json`'s margins and content margins, and returns null when it isn't installed or `UiStyle.withheld` is set (the harness's `cc0`); every builder then draws today's frame. `window()` wears its window (padding 0 or above 12) or panel (up to 12; the dock's `WOOD` plate stays drawn), `plank()`/`button()` its button states (a real pressed one), `focus()` its red chosen button, `UiStyle.tab(chosen)` and `UiStyle.tab_button(button, chosen)` its tabs, `UiStyle.slot(colourway, drawn)` its slots (`ready`, `spent`, `focus`, `fine`, `epic`: the dock's skills, what's worn), `UiStyle.bar_frame()` the boss bar's frame. Its buttons and tabs have page faces, so words on them take `UiStyle.plank_ink(lit)` (ink and rubric red with the kit, cream and gold without), never `CREAM`/`GOLD` by hand. Its frames are thicker (a window's words sit 22 in, a button is 31 tall at least, a slot 60 round a 32 px icon): check a screen with the kit and with `cc0` (`test_ui_kit`; the `fit-*` flows both ways). Rows, cards, plates, keycaps, the tooltip, scrollbars and the cursor stay drawn.
- **Two text palettes, by surface**: on the page `INK`, `FADED`, and `LAMP` (rubric red) for focus; on the dark around windows (headings, subtitles, gold counts, footers, the title, anything over the world) `CREAM`, `DUSK`, `GOLD`. Rarity inks are `FINE` and `EPIC`. Content that isn't in a window sits on `UiStyle.page(rect)`, never on the backdrop. `UiStyle.window()` is a main window; `UiStyle.box(fill, rim, padding)` infers the rest (a `LAMP` rim means focus, `CARD` with padding ≥ 12 means a window, anything else is a card). Footers are `UiStyle.footer("Esc  close      W/S  choose", at)` (keycaps, on the dark). Don't hand-roll `StyleBoxFlat`s or colours.
- **Type is one pixel font, never below 2x** (Tom found the 1x print ugly and hard to read): `UiStyle.label(text, size, color)` gives Pixeloid Sans at 18 on the canvas (2x of its 9 px) for any size below 24, and 27 (3x) from 24. `UiStyle.strong` is the bold cut; `UiStyle.sized(control, size)` applies the same rule to buttons and fields; `UiStyle.heading` is the bold cut at 27 for screen titles (18 and up), 18 for sections. Only the title's logo and menu use `UiStyle.logo_font()` (Press Start 2P). `UiStyle.setup()` (first line of `world._ready`) makes the default font render fixed-size. Never set fractional or arbitrary font sizes, never add a second body font, and size pages and rows for 18 px text (about 10 px per character): when a line doesn't fit, move the detail out of the row rather than shrinking it. VT323 and blackletter were tried and dropped.
- **Tooltips, scrollbars and the mouse are the look's too (PIX-384)**: `UiStyle.setup()` puts the tooltip's slip (`UiStyle.tooltip()`, ink on parchment in brass) and the slim brass-on-wood scrollbar in Godot's default theme (the root's theme stops at a screen's CanvasLayer), and hands `Input` the brass arrow and hand (`UiStyle.cursor`, Images at 2x, hotspots `ARROW_TIP`/`HAND_TIP`). `UiStyle.button` takes the hand. A tooltip is a window that samples as its owner does: give the control with `tooltip_text` `texture_filter = TEXTURE_FILTER_NEAREST`, or its words blur.
- **A list keeps to its page (PIX-298)**: in a row the name gives way (`Layout.ellipsis`, or `Layout.shortened(label, room)` when the whole name must show elsewhere), tags, prices and keys keep their words and wrap past a share of the row (`Layout.narrowed`); a long list scrolls in a window on its page. `Layout.overflows` (every `fit-*` flow, `test_fit`) also fails a scrolling window or a free list pushed wider than it was laid out (`Layout.spills`), however far inside the canvas, and a screen's piece standing on one of its pieces marked `Layout.CLEAR` (`Layout.crowded`, PIX-373: the ledger's status line and keys, which its info card grew under; opt-in per screen). The ledger's info says the chosen row's word only, never every row's. French runs longer: check a screen with `--lang fr` (or `--pseudo`) too.

## The HUD

- **The HUD is `Hud` (`scripts/world_hud.gd`, `world.hud`)**; its dock (`scripts/hud_dock.gd`, `world.hud.dock`) is a wooden plate centred on the bottom edge: XP line along its top, portrait/name/rank and HP + energy bars, six skill slots by number key, gold and a Menu that lists every screen with its key and lights when points wait. Nothing else sits at the bottom. `world.hud.dock.refresh()` after anything the hero shows changes. `--screenshot dockmenu` opens its menu. The place's nameplate near a shop door is `Hud.update_nameplate`; the clock and the quill sit top right (`saves.md`).
- **The marks over the world are pixel art in that palette (`Marks`, PIX-284, PIX-289)**: the talk mark over a giver (`Npc.mark_sprite`: a bubble on a pin's tail, "!"/"?", parchment, gold for the main story by `Quests.mark_tells_story`, faded while under way), the danger burst over a foe that notices (`Enemy.alert_mark`, cold blue for a fright), and the HUD's arrow toward the bearing (`Marks.arrow`, drawn for eight ways and never rotated, at UI_SCALE). They're rows of letters drawn at 1x, ringed in `NIGHT`, lit from the top left; centre them on heads by their middle columns (`Npc.mark_spot`, `Marks.centred`, `Ink`). Don't go back to font glyphs or turned polygons over the world.

## Words on the screen (`TextBudget`, PIX-334, PIX-343)

- **One rule** (`scripts/state/text_budget.gd`, pure, tested): an area of the screen shows at most `room` things at a time and the rest wait their turn (nothing is pushed off unread); the same thing again folds into the one showing or waiting ("+3 gold" four times reads "+12 gold", a quest's count takes the newest); in a fight what can wait (`later`: a quest's count, a level, the Post) waits until it's over, and while a title card is up (PIX-378: at a boss's fall the level's plate makes way for the Victory card, `Messages.make_way`, LEVEL UP rising saying it meanwhile). `Messages` (`world_messages.gd`) keeps the battle log (bottom-left, one short line at a time since PIX-343, each held `UiStyle.reading_seconds`) and the message plate through it.
- Words over the world step up just clear of a level tag or a word already there (`TextBudget.rise`, PIX-340: a pack shows one level tag, its highest; with no room within its most, back down to the lowest clear height, PIX-378), under the HUD's plates at the top (`room_under`). A kill's gold, XP and loot rise where it fell (`Gains`, `world_fx.gd`; PIX-245, PIX-343), LEVEL UP over the hero. A title card stands clear of what rises (`TextBudget.spot`, `Hud.title_card`, PIX-345).
- Hints wait their turn: never over a boss's entrance or a title card, one at a time (PIX-341); the harness's `hints` gives a run a new player's (on, none seen yet).

## The loading screen (PIX-296)

`assets/splash.png`, drawn in code by `tools/splash.py` (the desktop boot splash and the web page's picture), and the export's shell `web/shell.html` (its life: twinkles, embers, chimney smoke, windows lighting with the download, the dock's bar; `window.pixelheimShown` from `world.gd` lets it go); a launch's title rises out of the picture under its name. Change the title's logo or night and rerun `splash.py` (`--check` says when it's stale); `test_loading` pins the logo to the title's and the page to no words.

## Input

- **Never poll `Input.is_action_just_pressed` for a key a menu or conversation can take** (world, attack, skills): a modal that closes on a key event unpauses the tree in that same frame, and the poll still sees the key, so E reopened every conversation and Esc opened the pause menu behind it (PIX-131). Take keys in `_unhandled_input` and mark them handled. Polling is only for held input (`Input.get_vector`).
- Keys live in `Controls` (defaults, alternates, pad) and `GameSettings.bindings`; never `InputMap.add_action` elsewhere. InputMap bindings are for all devices (`Controls.ALL_DEVICES`). A new screen's hotkey is a new `Controls.BINDABLE` entry.

## Sound

Sounds are WAVs in `godot/assets/audio/` listed in `assets/data/audio.json`: the first ones rendered once from the old web synth, the newer by `tools/synth.py` (PIX-158, seeded); play them by name (`Sound.play("coin")`). New sounds: synthesize them in `sound.gd` like `UI_SOUNDS`, or render a WAV and add its entry. Music crossfades per place (`world_soundscape.gd`).
- **Levels**: every file has a dB in audio.json's `levels` (`Sound.level_of`, keyed by its path under `assets/audio/` without a variant's number), applied before the player's buses. The renders came out at whatever loudness their synth gave, so a new one is measured (`ffmpeg -af ebur128=peak=true`: integrated LUFS for music, the 400 ms max for a short effect) and levelled to its family: music at -37.5 LUFS, effects beside the ones played in the same moment, peaks under -1 dBTP. `tools/test_audio.py` refuses a file without a level and a `Sound.play`/`play_ui`/`play_track`/`play_theme` name that doesn't exist (Sound skips those silently).
