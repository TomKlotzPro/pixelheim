# The newcomer

**Goal:** be a first-time player. Start from the title, make a hero, and play the first half hour. Find every moment a new player would be lost, bored or wrong-footed.

**Start:** a plain launch: `start` with no option, in English. A fresh profile, so the game is new to you.

**Model:** Sonnet. This player needs judgment more than anything else.

## Route

1. **The title.** Watch the entrance once without pressing anything: 2 or 3 looks. Then choose New Game.
2. **Making a hero.** Read every choice before you pick. Pick a warrior unless your brief says otherwise.
3. **The opening scene and the Night of Ash.** Follow what the game asks. Read every box and say whether you understood it.
4. **The dawn and the village.** Find out what to do next from what the game shows you: the "Next" line on the dock, the arrow, the journal (Q). Don't guess from what you know of the game.
5. **The first quests.** Take them, do them, hand them in. Fight what the quests send you to.
6. **If you fall**, see where you wake and whether you understand why.

Stop when your looks run out, or when you have handed in two quests outside the village.

## Check, all along

- **At every moment, do I know what to do next?** Count the looks you spend unsure. More than three in a row is a confusing finding: say what you looked at and what would have told you.
- **Is each box short enough to read?** A box you had to scroll, or one of more than about four lines, is a polish finding. Quote it.
- **Do the hints help, or get in the way?**
- **Can I find the quest's place?** Do the arrow and the journal agree with where the quest really is?
- **Is the first fight fair?** Can I see the foe's attack coming? Do my hits land when I think they should?
- **Do I notice what I won?** Gold, XP, a new item, a level.
- **Does anything ask for a key without saying which one?**

Also report any bug you meet on the way, as `SKILL.md` describes.
