# The thunderstorms' flows (PIX-491: a feature's flows, read after
# tools/flows.txt as its own, scripts/features/storm/), a line each as in
# flows.txt.
# A thunderstorm at its height over the Reach by day: the ground soaked,
# the puddles full, and a strike's flash held, the sky's light throwing
# shadows. At night on the City's square the same. Under the inn's roof
# the storm is heard and the windows flash: no puddles, nothing cast. With
# Reduce motion a strike is the soft kind, with no shadows. Five strikes
# at once show three flashes, no more (the cap).
# A bolt casts only with the Shadows setting on (off by default since
# 2026-11-18): the day and night strikes turn it on (`shadows`).
storm-day | --map overworld storm shadows --strike held | map=overworld storm=rain 1\.0 flashes 1 puddles 100 wet 1\.0 cast | field
storm-night | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night storm shadows --strike held | map=town storm=rain 1\.0 flashes 1 puddles 100 wet 1\.0 cast | town night
storm-inside | --map town_inn night storm --strike now | map=town_inn storm=inside rain 1\.0 flashes 1 puddles 0 wet 0\.0 | rooms night
storm-still | --map overworld storm --strike now still | map=overworld storm=rain 1\.0 flashes 1 puddles 100 wet 1\.0 soft | field
storm-cap | --map overworld storm --strike 5 | map=overworld storm=rain 1\.0 flashes 3 puddles 100 wet 1\.0( cast)? | field
# Half an hour after the storm the ground is half dry and the puddles
# are shrinking, no rain falling.
storm-drying | --map overworld drying | map=overworld storm=rain 0\.0 flashes 0 puddles 6[67] wet 0\.5 | field
