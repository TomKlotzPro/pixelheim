# Light relief's flows (PIX-528: a feature's flows, read after
# tools/flows.txt as its own, scripts/features/relief/), a line each as in
# flows.txt.
# The City's square at night with Light relief forced on: the plain pieces
# drawn (props, lamp posts, stalls, crates) wear its material; forced off,
# they still do (the shaders light them as before), and it says off.
relief-town | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night --relief on | map=town relief=on pieces [1-9]\d* | town night
relief-off | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night --relief off | map=town relief=off pieces [1-9]\d* | town night
# PIX-529: the depth from the art's forms. The forge at night, its floor
# and its plaster walls flat (nothing carved there: its 3-px brick rows
# were a corrugation), its furniture domed; the Frostgate's observatory
# yard between its lamps, its flags carved, its edge stones and the snow
# by them plain. Both hold only that the pieces are dressed: the pictures
# hold the look.
relief-room | --map town_smith night --relief on | map=town_smith relief=on pieces [1-9]\d* | rooms night
relief-frost | --map frostgate --at 27,9 night --relief on | map=frostgate relief=on pieces [1-9]\d* | field night
# PIX-531: the lights' rim on what stands, with the relief on or off: whole
# at night, none by day (outdoors, and a room's dim day at the run's first
# hour, six in the morning), held back by --rim off. The pictures hold the
# look.
rim-night | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night | map=town rim=on 1\.00 | town night
rim-day | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 | map=town rim=on 0\.00 | town
rim-off | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night --rim off | map=town rim=off 0\.00 | town night
rim-room-day | --map town_smith | map=town_smith rim=on 0\.00 | rooms
