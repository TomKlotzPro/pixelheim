# The lamps' flows (PIX-361: a feature's flows, read after tools/flows.txt
# as its own, scripts/features/lamps/), a line each as in flows.txt.
# The land heals round a waystation's ember lamp (PIX-323): two mornings
# after it was lit, bare earth and grass a couple of cells round it, the
# hero four cells off still in the Ash's air; five mornings on, the whole
# ring, bare earth at its edge to saplings by the lamp, and the hero inside
# it in the open land's air, by day and at night.
lamps-heal-early | --map overworld --routes blackiron:6 --built waystation_blackiron --lit blackiron:2 --at 25,22 | map=overworld healed=earth\d+,grass\d+,meadow\d+,grove0 air=ash | travel field
lamps-heal-whole | --map overworld --routes blackiron:6 --built waystation_blackiron --lit blackiron:5 --at 25,22 | map=overworld healed=earth\d+,grass\d+,meadow\d+,grove\d+ air=open | travel field
lamps-heal-night | --map overworld --routes blackiron:6 --built waystation_blackiron --lit blackiron:5 --at 25,22 night | healed=earth\d+,grass\d+,meadow\d+,grove\d+ air=open waystations=blackiron:lit | travel field night
# The fields renamed as they heal (PIX-325): every ring on the ash whole,
# and walking down off the mountain's foot into them the card says the
# Lantern Meadows.
fields-renamed | --map overworld --built waystation_blackiron,waystation_greyhold,waystation_frostgate --lit blackiron:5,greyhold:5,frostgate:5 --at 48,7 --walk d,d --wait 0.4 --lang en | map=overworld card=The Lantern Meadows | travel field
# Lamp kinds by region (PIX-324): the Saltmere road's lamp in sea glass
# reaching a cell further, the Frostgate road's frost lily as far as an
# ember's, and the grave moss lamp at the Mirefen Pass, the hero by it out
# of the Mirefen's fog, which rolls in as before with no lamp there; each
# burning in its own colour at night; Aske home, every ring a third wider;
# and a healed lamp's morning find by it and Bram out from the village.
