# The Mirefen alive (PIX-401): dead trees, reed beds, six boardwalks and
# two stilt huts stand in the fen by day, the wisps dark; at night the
# wisps are out; the middle road goes on west as a boardwalk the hero walks
# along to the west hut, which blocks the way into it; he wades off its
# planks into the fen's shallows along its sides (PIX-519), drawn shallow
# and waded there, his feet sunk, and nowhere on the planks (PIX-548).
mire-fen | --map mirefen --at 30,20 | map=mirefen mire=trees [3-9]\d reeds \d{2,3} walks 6 huts 2 wisps 9 dark | field
mire-night | --map mirefen --at 30,20 night | map=mirefen mire=trees [3-9]\d reeds \d{2,3} walks 6 huts 2 wisps 9 lit | field night
mire-boardwalk | --map mirefen --at 12,24 --walk l,l,l,l,l | map=mirefen cell=\([4-9], 24\) wade= | field
mire-boardwalk-water | --map mirefen --at 9,25 --walk d | map=mirefen cell=\(9, 26\) wade=2 water=shallows still | field
mire-hut | --map mirefen --at 5,24 --walk u,u | map=mirefen cell=\(5, 24\) | field
