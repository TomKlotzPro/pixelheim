# The night shadows' flows (PIX-490: a feature's flows, read after
# tools/flows.txt as its own, scripts/features/night_shadows/), a line each
# as in flows.txt.
# At night the lamps nearest the hero cast shadows: on the City's square,
# where far more lamps burn than may cast, the cap of three, no more, a
# second after arriving (they fade in);
# occluders at the feet of what stands and round the houses' feet and the
# walls. By day none casts (the sun's shadows alone, PIX-449). In the
# Deepwood the camps' fires near it; in the sea cave its wall torches, the
# rock round its floors blocking them. The hero's lantern never casts (its
# shadows read as a field of view, Tom 2026-11-18), and the Shadows setting
# is off by default: these runs turn it on (`shadows`).
night-shadows-town | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 night shadows --wait 1 | map=town nightshadows=lights 3 of 3 lamps (?![0-3] )\d+ feet [1-9]\d* walls [1-9]\d* | town night
night-shadows-day | --map town --town-tier 4 --at 40,24 | map=town nightshadows=lights 0 of 3 lamps 0 feet [1-9]\d* walls [1-9]\d* | town
night-shadows-wood | --map deepwood night shadows --wait 1 | map=deepwood nightshadows=lights [0-3] of 3 lamps \d+ feet [1-9]\d* walls \d+ | field night
night-shadows-cave | --map seacave shadows --wait 1 | map=seacave nightshadows=lights [1-3] of 3 lamps \d+ feet \d+ walls [1-9]\d* | dungeon
