Fonts for the Godot build (scripts/ui_style.gd).

- Nunito-Regular.ttf, Nunito-Bold.ttf  Nunito by Vernon Adams, Jacques Le
  Bailly and the Nunito Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence
  in Nunito-OFL.txt), via fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito (the static cuts
  of Google Fonts' download, unchanged). Every word read: the dialogue, the
  menus, the HUD, the journal, the shops. Drawn smooth at whole pixels, 20 on
  the canvas and 30 for screen titles, its line box cut to Pixeloid's (22,
  33) so the pages keep their room (PIX-533). Nunito-Regular.ttf is the
  project's default font (gui/theme/custom_font).
- PixeloidSans(-Bold).ttf  Pixeloid by GGBotNet, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (licence in Pixeloid-LICENSE.txt), via ggbot.itch.io/pixeloid-font. A pixel
  font, drawn at its own 9 px and scaled by whole numbers only: the numbers
  and words floating over the world, the keycaps' letters, and the arrows
  Nunito lacks (its fallback, ← ↑ → ↓).
- press-start-2p.woff2     Press Start 2P by CodeMan38, SIL Open Font License
  1.1. The PIXELHEIM logo only (UiStyle.logo_font).
