Fonts for the Godot build. All are pixel fonts, rendered at their own pixel
size and scaled by whole numbers only (scripts/ui_style.gd).

- PixelOperator*.ttf     Pixel Operator by Jayvee Enaguas, CC0 1.0 (licence in
  PixelOperator-LICENSE.txt), via dafont.com/pixel-operator.font. The body
  type: PixelOperator(-Bold) at 16px for dense text (rows, descriptions,
  hints), PixelOperator8(-Bold) at 2x for what is read at a glance
  (dialogue, names, menus, values). PixelOperator.ttf is the project's
  default font (gui/theme/custom_font).
- press-start-2p.woff2   Press Start 2P by CodeMan38, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  Synced from the web game (src/assets/fonts) by scripts/sync-godot-assets.mjs.
  Headings and the logo, as on the web.
