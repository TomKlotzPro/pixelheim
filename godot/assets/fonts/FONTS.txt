Fonts for the Godot build. Both are pixel fonts, rendered at their own pixel
size and scaled by whole numbers only (scripts/ui_style.gd), so every letter
sits on the same 2x grid as the frames around it.

- PixeloidSans(-Bold).ttf  Pixeloid by GGBotNet, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (licence in Pixeloid-LICENSE.txt), via ggbot.itch.io/pixeloid-font. All the
  game's text: drawn at 9 px, shown at 18 (2x) for what's read and 27 (3x)
  for screen titles. PixeloidSans.ttf is the project's default font
  (gui/theme/custom_font).
- press-start-2p.woff2     Press Start 2P by CodeMan38, SIL Open Font License
  1.1. The title's logo and menu (UiStyle.logo_font).
