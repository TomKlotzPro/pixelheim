Universal (public domain): free for any use, commercial or not, modification
and redistribution allowed, no attribution required. The author states no
generative AI was used.

- characters/  "Free 16x16 Puny Character Sprites"   https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-characters
- beasts/      "PunyMonsters" (same page)
- mini/        "Free 16x16 Mini World Sprites"       https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-mini-world-sprites
- world/       "Free 16x16 Puny World Tileset"       https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-world
- dungeon/     "Free 16x16 Puny Dungeon Tileset"     https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-dungeon

Files are unmodified copies, the Tiled tilesets (.tsx) included (their image
paths point one folder up, as in Shade's zips; Pixelheim reads only the wang
and animation data). Pixelheim picks which sheet plays which role in
scripts/puny_art.gd and lays the tilesets over its maps in
scripts/puny_terrain.gd.

Not in this repository: medieval/ holds Shade's paid "16x16 Puny World
Tileset Medieval Age" (https://merchant-shade.itch.io/puny-world-medieval),
licensed to Tom Klotz for use in commercial projects, modification allowed,
no redistribution or resale. It is fetched from the private
TomKlotzPro/pixelheim-assets repository (pnpm godot:art locally, a deploy key
in CI) and git-ignored here; scripts/puny_town.gd builds the town's houses
from it.
