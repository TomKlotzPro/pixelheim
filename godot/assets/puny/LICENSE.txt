Files are unmodified copies, the Tiled tilesets (.tsx) included, except
two looks made from his sheets by swapping colours (godot/tools/outfits.py,
for worn gear): characters/Archer-Leather.png (the green archer in leather
browns) and characters/Warrior-Wyrm.png (the warrior's helm blackened and
gilded). Unmodified otherwise (their image
paths point one folder up, as in Shade's zips; Pixelheim reads only the wang
and animation data). Pixelheim picks which sheet plays which role in
scripts/puny_art.gd and lays the tilesets over its maps in
scripts/puny_terrain.gd.

Not in this repository, Shade's paid packs, licensed to Tom Klotz for use in
commercial projects, modification allowed, no redistribution or resale.
They live in the private TomKlotzPro/pixelheim-assets repository under
retro-rpg/shade/, are fetched by scripts/fetch-private-art.sh (run it
locally; a deploy key in CI) and git-ignored here:
